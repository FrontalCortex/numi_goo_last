Part I — Privacy Policy

1. Data Controller

For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, the Data Controller is:

  • Data Controller: Başar Melih Tümtürk
  • Contact Email: sorobit.support@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, maintain, and improve our educational services.

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: when you register, either manually or via Google Sign-In, we collect your display name, username, email address, and account role (student or teacher).
  • Learning & progress data: abacus lesson scores, lesson completion, virtual balances (gold, keys, energy), badges, mission progress, and leaderboard rankings.
  • User-generated content: if you use the App's features to communicate with teachers or submit questions, we process the chat messages, questions, audio recordings, screen recordings, or image attachments you choose to provide.
  • Age or birth year: collected solely to apply age-appropriate advertising treatment and to comply with applicable child-privacy laws (such as COPPA and GDPR).

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Usage & diagnostics: app interaction events and crash/stability data (Firebase Analytics).
  • Advertising identifier: used by Google AdMob to serve and verify ads (see Section 3).
  • Push notification token: used to deliver notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Device and session data: device model, OS version, and active session count, used to enforce single/limited concurrent device sign-in.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as under the GDPR or KVKK), we process your information based on:

  • Performance of a contract: to create your account, save your progress, and provide the App's core functionality.
  • Legitimate interests: to maintain the security, stability, and technical performance of the App, and to prevent abuse (e.g. cheating or duplicate accounts).
  • Consent: where required by law, including for personalized advertising.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

5. Children's Privacy

Sorobit is an educational application intended primarily for middle school, high school, and university students, as well as other general learners practicing mental arithmetic. While the App is not specifically directed at young children, we recognize that our audience may include users under the age of digital consent, which varies by jurisdiction.

  • Users who declare (or are otherwise identified as) under 13 years old are shown non-personalized advertising only; personalized ads are enabled only for users who declare a sufficient age.
  • We limit data collection from children to what is reasonably necessary to operate the educational service.
  • The teacher-approval mechanism encourages student accounts to be used under adult supervision.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data in a manner not permitted by applicable law, contact us at sorobit.support@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as reasonably necessary to provide the service. If you delete your account, we take commercially reasonable steps to delete or anonymize your personal data, except where retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In-app deletion: open the App, go to your Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account, and confirm.
  • Deletion without the app: if you no longer have the App installed, email sorobit.support@gmail.com from the address registered on your account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will verify your identity and delete your account and associated data within 30 days.

Deletion removes your profile, progress, wallet balances, and messages. Some information may be retained where required by law (e.g. transaction records for tax purposes) or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your applicable local laws, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw previously granted consent. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 1 or Section 19.

14. Purchases, Subscriptions, and Refunds

Sorobit offers optional in-app purchases (consumable gold and key packs) and auto-renewing subscriptions (Pro and Lite plans) processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details.

  • Consumable purchases grant a fixed, server-determined amount of gold or keys; the amount is never taken from the client device.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period unless canceled at least 24 hours before renewal, through your Google Play account subscription settings.
  • Refund requests are handled according to Google Play's refund policies. Where a purchase is refunded or a chargeback occurs, we reserve the right to reverse the associated virtual items from your account, which may result in a negative balance that must be cleared before further balance-increasing purchases or rewards apply.
